ColorLenz / MoodLenz – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

What We Collect
ColorLenz / MoodLenz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app captures your screen locally on your device solely to apply visual filters in real time. No screen content ever leaves your device.

Screen Recording
ColorLenz / MoodLenz requests Screen Recording permission to display a filtered live preview of your screen. This data is processed entirely on-device and is never saved, uploaded, or shared.

Subscription & Payments
Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. For billing inquiries, visit Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device.

Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children
ColorLenz / MoodLenz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes
We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
